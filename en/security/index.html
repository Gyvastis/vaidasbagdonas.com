FAQ

Common questions

Is my website really hacked?

Common signs: a Google warning, unfamiliar pages or links on your site, redirects to other addresses, spam sent in your name, changed files. Send me the address – I look at what is publicly visible for free and tell you what I noticed. I can only establish the exact cause with access and your written consent.

What should I do right now while I wait?

Do not change or delete files, so evidence is not lost. Make a copy of the site and the database (in your hosting panel). Change passwords wherever you can: hosting, email, CMS admin. You do not need to send me any passwords.

How long will it take?

I reply within 1 working day. We agree the scope and price in writing before I start, and I give a timeframe once I see what has to be done. If it is urgent, ask about emergency help.

Do I need to give you passwords?

I will need access, but not through chat. We agree a secure way to hand it over in writing, and change the passwords when the work is done.

Can you do a penetration test?

No. I am not a certified penetration tester and I do not run such tests. I do a security check of versions, updates, backups and access – only with your written consent. If you need a pentest, I will tell you what to look for.
